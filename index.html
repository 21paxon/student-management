About Our System

The Student Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify the administration of student information. It helps educational institutions manage student records, reduce paperwork, save time, improve data accuracy, and make information retrieval faster and more reliable.

Student Registration

Register new students and capture their personal information in an organized way.

Student Records

Easy Access
